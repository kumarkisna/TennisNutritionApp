---
name: Goal-review reminder policy
description: Safety and persistence boundaries for reminders about user-entered nutrient goals.
---

Treat older professional-review dates as opportunities for an optional reminder, never evidence that a goal is medically valid, invalid, or expired. The twelve-month interval is a product reminder convention, not a clinical schedule.

**Why:** The request calls for an unobtrusive, dismissible prompt without medical inference or required demographic details; there is no supplied clinical review interval.

**How to apply:** Keep date provenance and the nonclinical interval clear. Do not invent dates when reviews are absent or change intake values automatically. Browser-local dismissal should be scoped to account, person, nutrient, saved value and saved review date, so one dismissal does not suppress a changed goal or another person's reminder.
