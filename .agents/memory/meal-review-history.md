---
name: Meal review history
description: Product decision for how meal ratings, notes, and reuse relate to planner dates.
---

Store meal ratings, user keywords, and notes against a specific person and date. A calendar should show those dated reviews and open that day's planned meals and tennis details. Reuse defaults to meal portions only. An explicit full-tennis-plan choice also copies sessions, fuel notes, timed food slots, and linked portions; copied session times and durations are editable.

**Why:** The user chose dated history, then explicitly opted into copying the complete tennis plan when desired, while retaining control over session timing and duration.

**How to apply:** Keep meal reviews and tennis experience ratings attached to the source date and never copy either score. Preserve destination meals and activity/target settings; do not overwrite an existing tennis plan. Active tennis sessions require an active destination activity. A meals-only copy must not carry tennis scheduling.
