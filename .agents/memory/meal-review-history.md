---
name: Meal review history
description: Product decision for how meal ratings, notes, and reuse relate to planner dates.
---

Store meal ratings, user keywords, and notes against a specific person and date. A calendar should show those dated reviews and open that day's planned meals. Reusing a day copies its meal portions only; it does not move the review, rating, or tennis schedule.

**Why:** The user chose a dated history with reuse instead of a standalone meal-combination catalog, so a rating remains tied to the actual day it describes.

**How to apply:** Keep meal reviews distinct from tennis-plan experience ratings. When copying meals to another date or person, preserve destination data and leave the source review attached to its original day.
