---
name: Daily meal timing defaults
description: Why meal defaults are suggestions and assigned day times must stay stable.
---

Treat default breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack times as editable scheduling suggestions, not universally optimal or personalised health advice.

**Why:** The user requested automatic starting times, but individual waking times, routines, appetite and training schedules are not established. Clock times alone do not justify changing nutrition targets.

**How to apply:** Keep defaults clearly editable and separate from intake recommendations. Changes apply to the selected person and date; tennis-specific food times remain independent.

Keep assigned times stable for a saved food day, even if future routine defaults change.

**Why:** Automatic defaults should not retroactively retime foods the user already planned. A suggested default and an assigned day time have different lifecycles.

**How to apply:** Preserve the day's assigned times when editing food, activity or tennis plans. Any future recurring-routine feature should apply new defaults to new days unless the user explicitly chooses to retime existing plans.
