---
name: Weight-loss nutrition goal semantics
description: The user's decision to track weight loss as per-person intent without changing nutrition estimates.
---

Track weight loss as an optional per-person nutrition goal, not as a physical activity or a calorie prescription.

**Why:** The user chose to record the goal only. The app's activity-based estimates and nutrition references should remain unchanged.

**How to apply:** Preserve the goal with its person's profile and keep it separate from daily activity and target calculations. Do not infer a calorie deficit or weight-loss recommendation from this goal.
