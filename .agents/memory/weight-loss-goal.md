---
name: Weight-loss nutrition goal semantics
description: The user's decision to track weight loss as per-person intent without changing nutrition estimates.
---

Track weight loss as an optional per-person nutrition goal, not as a physical activity or calorie prescription. Dated weigh-ins and the optional target weight are progress-only; they must not change planning body weight, calorie estimates, or nutrition references.

**Why:** The user chose to record the goal only and later selected a progress log with an optional target. These records are informational and must stay separate from the body weight used for planning.

**How to apply:** Preserve the goal, target, and dated weigh-ins with their person's profile. Keep them separate from daily activity and target calculations. Do not infer a calorie deficit or weight-loss recommendation from these fields.
